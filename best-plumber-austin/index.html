What to have ready before service

The work may involve maintaining existing plumbing, installing equipment, repairing a problem, or upgrading a system. The right approach depends on the situation and should follow an assessment of the issue.

A short preparation list can make the call more useful:

  • Write down when it began and whether it has changed.
  • Mention any repair attempts or earlier work you know about.
  • Make the affected area accessible if you can do so safely.
  • Keep your questions about the proposed work in one place.
